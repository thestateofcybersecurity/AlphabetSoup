# Security policy

This repository builds [www.cybersecurityalphabetsoup.com](https://www.cybersecurityalphabetsoup.com), a static site with one server-side feature (the ransomware alert subscription, which lives in a separate Cloudflare Worker). The site has no accounts and stores nothing about visitors beyond that opt-in subscription, so most findings will be client-side (escaping, imports, exports) or supply-chain (dependencies, workflows).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately first. Either channel works:

- Email: parker@cybersecurityalphabetsoup.com (the same address published at `/.well-known/security.txt` and on the site's legal pages).
- GitHub: [open a private security advisory](https://github.com/thestateofcybersecurity/AlphabetSoup/security/advisories/new) on this repository.

Please include what you found, where (URL, file, or commit), steps to reproduce, and what you believe the impact is. A proof of concept against a local build (`npm run build && npm run preview`) is welcome; please do not test against other people's data, run volumetric tests, or send email to addresses you do not control through the alert form.

You will get an acknowledgment within 48 hours and a status update within 7 days. Fixes for anything that lets script run on the site origin, exposes subscriber data, or compromises the build pipeline are treated as the next thing to ship.

## Scope

In scope:

- Everything served from `www.cybersecurityalphabetsoup.com` and the apex, including the client-side tools, imports and exports, and the content pipeline in `scripts/`.
- The GitHub Actions workflows, the dependency manifest, and anything that could tamper with the published build.
- The companion hosts under `cybersecurityalphabetsoup.com` (alerts, bia, lantern, mitre, cyberdle) are owned by the same person; reports about them are welcome here too and will be routed to the right repository.

Out of scope: findings that require a victim to paste hostile data into their own browser storage with no way to deliver it (the site treats its own localStorage as untrusted, but a report needs a delivery path), clickjacking of pages with nothing to click, and reports generated by scanners without a demonstrated impact.

## Disclosure

Coordinated disclosure, with a default of 90 days from acknowledgment to public disclosure, shortened whenever the fix ships sooner. Reporters are credited in the fixing commit or release note if they want to be. Good-faith research that follows this policy will not be met with legal action.

## What is already in place

- Supported version: the site is continuously deployed from `main`; there are no versioned releases to patch, and the live site always reflects the newest green build.
- Text and attribute contexts are escaped separately (`src/lib/escape.ts`), stored state is validated on load, and the news renderer treats its feed as hostile by design.
- Actions are pinned to commit SHAs, `npm audit` gates every deploy, Semgrep runs weekly with results in code scanning, and Dependabot keeps actions and dependencies current.
- `/.well-known/security.txt` publishes this contact information in machine-readable form.
